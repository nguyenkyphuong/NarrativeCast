# NarrativeCast — ND3 Task 5: Cluster Quality Review

**Run evaluated:** ND3.4b scope v2, MiniBatchKMeans, K=24, seed=42; 7,244 assigned POSTs reported by technical acceptance (13/13 PASS). **Review data actually examined here:** 360 genuine text rows in `cluster_labeling_samples (1).csv`, exactly 15 per cluster (5 CORE + 5 MIDDLE + 5 EDGE); 24 temporary descriptors from `nd3_task4b_v2_temporary_labels.csv`. No full 7,244-row assignment or raw 94 embedding shards was available to recompute cluster-level population statistics in this review.

## 1. Purpose, boundary and methodology

This document covers **only ND3 Task 5** as specified in the assignment workbook: sample large and small clusters, check topical/stance/interpretation similarity, record noise and mixed narratives; deliver `reports/results/narrative_examples.csv` plus this report. It does **not** construct temporally stable narrative IDs, temporal windows, targets or forecasts (ND4).

**Evidence method:** centroid-stratified sample (5 core, 5 middle, 5 edge per cluster). The descriptive labels and first-pass semantic classes are **AI-assisted provisional review** of the supplied text. Rows marked `NARRATIVE_CANDIDATE` flag a possible claim or call for action, **not a validated recurring narrative**. Every row retains real `post_id`, full supplied `content`, UTC timestamp, cluster ID, sampling stratum, cosine centroid similarity, and explicit `UNRESOLVED` narrative ID. Stance defaults to `NOT_INFERRED` unless wording provides an unmistakable cue; even stance hints remain provisional. Some headlines report *someone else’s view*, so stance may not be the Reddit poster’s stance.

**Important limitation:** The sample was selected intentionally by centroid similarity, not by probability sampling. Thus proportions of `NARRATIVE_CANDIDATE`, `TOPIC_ONLY`, or `UNCERTAIN` in the 360 rows are **not** unbiased cluster purity/precision or population prevalence estimates. Cluster character labels were made provisionally in the preceding Task 4b review and are being critically re-used, not independently confirmed by double annotation. No separate human annotator signed off.

## 2. Evidence and overall findings

- **Sample completeness:** 360 POSTs; 24/24 clusters; each has 5 CORE, 5 MIDDLE and 5 EDGE; unique post IDs: True.
- **Provisional cluster character:** 12 `COHERENT_TOPIC`, 10 `MIXED`, 2 `NOISY_MIXED` (qualitative counts, not validated purity).
- **First-pass text category** `TOPIC_ONLY`: 189/360 samples (heuristic review tally only).
- **First-pass text category** `UNCERTAIN`: 124/360 samples (heuristic review tally only).
- **First-pass text category** `NARRATIVE_CANDIDATE`: 47/360 samples (heuristic review tally only).
- Comparison from ND3.4b v2: mean cosine silhouette K12=0.03776, K24=0.05143, K32=0.05084; mean seed ARI K12=0.38081, K24=0.40327, K32=0.39094. K24 selected using predeclared weighted ranking. Low absolute silhouette and modest ARI should be disclosed, not recast as narrative accuracy.
- Examples expose within-cluster **event-only content**, **claims with different policy directions**, and **organizational or country-specific topics**. There is no justified one-cluster-one-narrative mapping.

## 4. Detailed interpretation, noise and narrative mixing

- **Clear topic/event clusters do not automatically contain a single narrative.** Court rulings, California legislation, Title II classifications, and FCC public-comment procedures group a common subject even when the supplied posts support opposite implications or simply report news.
- **Mixed clusters** contain different claim targets and actions. For example, public mobilization and procedural news can co-occur; telecom-company behavior can be discussed from consumer-rights, market, regulatory and political perspectives. This is observed diversity of argument, not automatically poor preprocessing.
- **Noisy/mixed clusters 6 and 14** were already flagged in the provisional Task4b review; preserve their membership and keep interpretations unresolved. Recheck outliers and near-centroid counterexamples rather than assigning an invented coherent narrative.
- **Explicit counterexamples** include posts in a legal cluster that report court action without asserting normative consequences, as well as Title II posts arguing both for and against regulatory action. See individual `post_id` rows, `interpretation_type`, and notes in the companion CSV.
- **Duplicate / event amplification:** earlier exact-normalized duplicate audit counted 124 duplicate-member posts within 7,096 original-scope assignments (not recalculated for v2); later v2 scope differs. Repeated headlines can exaggerate apparent topical coherence. Do not use that older count as the v2 duplicate rate.

## 5. Decision and limitations

**Task 5 assessment: COMPLETE AS AI-ASSISTED DESCRIPTIVE REVIEW, NOT GOLD-LABEL ANNOTATION.** Required artifacts are populated with real examples and systematic samples, observed mixing/noise, and explicit limits. The result is suitable for exploratory reporting and to inform ND4 protocol decisions. It does **not** authorize treating 24 cluster IDs as 24 stable narratives.

**Limitations to carry forward:** 7,244 records are a keyword-defined + AI-reviewed scope; 148 near-miss additions came from an expansion screen, which does not establish recall of the entire corpus. Only 360 sampled texts were assessed here; no population-valid purity confidence interval; single AI-assisted review; many posts are headline-length; implicit stance/claim may be ambiguous; event recurrence and cross-cluster narrative overlap unquantified; retrospective 2014–2020 fitting poses lookahead risk if reused to test forecasts. Before predictive work, decide temporal-fitting/splitting protocol without looking into future windows.
